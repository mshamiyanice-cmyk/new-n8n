## Gorilla Trekking Information

### Permit Information

#### Pricing
- Foreign non-residents: $1,500 per person
- Foreign residents: $500 per person
- Rwandans and East African citizens: $200 per person
- **Special Discount**: 30% discount on gorilla permit if visiting during November to May AND spending at least two nights at other Rwanda national parks

#### Permit Details
- Rwanda issues a maximum of 96 gorilla permits per day
- Permits should be booked 6 months in advance
- Available through Rwanda Development Board (RDB) or local tour operators

#### What the Permit Covers
- Entrance to Volcanoes National Park
- Guided trek
- Maximum of one hour with the gorillas

#### What's NOT Included in Permit
- Porter fees
- Tips to trackers and guides
- Accommodations
- Transport
- Meals
- Other activities

### Requirements for Gorilla Trekking

#### Age Requirement
- Minimum age: 15 years (strictly enforced)

#### Health Requirements
- Maintain a distance of 7 meters from gorillas
- If sick with cold, flu, or other contagious illness, DO NOT visit the gorillas
- Distance requirement is for gorilla health protection

#### Required Documents
- Valid gorilla permit
- Passport (for ID verification at pre-trekking briefing)

#### Arrival Instructions
- Present yourself at RDB Tourism & Conservation offices in Kinigi at 7:00 AM on trekking day
- Attend pre-trekking briefing

### What to Pack for Gorilla Trekking

#### Essential Items
- Good camera
- Insect repellents
- Sunscreen
- Hiking boots
- Hand gloves
- Binoculars
- Rain jacket

#### Clothing Recommendations
- Layered clothes (mornings are cool, can be hot during hiking)
- Moisture-wicking clothing
- Prepare for mist and rain
- Night time can be cold

### Booking & Cancellation Policies

#### Booking Timeline
- Book at least 6 months in advance
- High demand for permits

#### Refund Policy
- No refund for late arrivals or flight delays
- No refund for absence or cancellation for any reason
- **Exception**: If unable to complete trek due to gorilla group's movement, guests receive another chance OR full refund

### Porter Services
- Available to help carry daypacks
- Small charge applies
- Highly recommended for comfort during trek

## Common Customer Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How much does gorilla trekking cost?
Gorilla permits cost $1,500 for foreign non-residents. There's a 30% discount during November-May if you spend 2+ nights at other Rwanda national parks.

### 2. When should I book my gorilla permit?
Book at least 6 months in advance. Rwanda issues only 96 permits per day.

### 3. What's the minimum age for gorilla trekking?
15 years old (strictly enforced).

### 4. What's included in the room rate?
All-inclusive: all meals, selected drinks, house wines, minibar, 45-minute massage, WiFi, cultural performances, and on-site activities.

### 5. How far is the lodge from Volcanoes National Park?
Conveniently located near the entrance of Volcanoes National Park.

### 6. What should I pack for gorilla trekking?
Hiking boots, rain jacket, layered moisture-wicking clothes, gloves, camera, insect repellent, sunscreen, and binoculars.

### 7. What time do I need to arrive for gorilla trekking?
7:00 AM at RDB Tourism & Conservation offices in Kinigi for the pre-trekking briefing.

### 8. Can I get a refund if I can't make it?
No refunds for late arrivals, flight delays, or cancellations. Exception: If trek fails due to gorilla group movement, you get another chance or full refund.

### 9. What other activities are available besides gorilla trekking?
Golden monkey tracking, Dian Fossey tomb hike, volcano hikes, cultural performances, walking tours, cycling, and visits to Ellen DeGeneres - Diane Fossey Gorilla Center.

### 10. Is the lodge family-friendly?
Yes, family rooms available that can accommodate two adults and one infant.
